Three moves, one loop

Open crates to collect cars, put money on a race, and watch the pot pay out to the cent.

01 · Open

Open a crate

Open a crate and see what you pull. The five rarity tiers drop at the same fixed odds every time — 50 / 28 / 14 / 6 / 2 percent.

02 · Bet

Bet on a race

Enter your car in a race and place a bet. Everyone's bets go into one shared pot. Don't own a car? You can still bet on someone else's.

03 · Win

Get paid

When the race ends, the pot pays out to the cent. If a car you own finishes on the podium, you take an extra slice.
